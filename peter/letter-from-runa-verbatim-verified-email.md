Sweden the 8th of August 2026

Dear Rajmohan Gandhi jee!

Before I started to write this letter, I tried to listen in silence. Of
course I wanted to find the right words - why else should I write at
all? But such a wish, when strong and insisting in the mind, can block
the way to real silence. That is what I learned a short while ago! My
ambition now is not to find the "right" words, but to write down what
genuinely comes to me, hopefully expressing what I wish and want - that
what I think you also wish and want. God bless!

I am 84 years old, mother, grandmother and lately even great
grandmother. In that quiet time I just had, I also experienced a
surprise: I suddenly felt the presence of my parents in the room. My
mother and my father left this world many, many years ago. Now I did not
see them, but clearly knew they were close! Their astonishing and
surprising visit had to do with this letter. Not that they said
anything, but I felt both their keen interest and support and was
encouraged.

I am almost sure that you met my parents Ingeborg and Gösta Ekman from
Sweden when you visited Caux in the 1950-is some time.

I grew up in the atmosphere of MRA after the second worldwar when the
movement made a great impact in Sweden, not least among the
intellectuals, the artists, the writers and the wealthy. As a family we
spent week-ends at the MRA-center, a beautiful mansion in the capital
city of Stockholm, where we met the full-time workers who lived there
and took part in the house-meetings, the conferences, the quiet times -
the sharing and the plans to change the world by changing ourselves and
follow Truth. As a child or even as a teenager I never understood
anything of it. My parents were deeply engaged for many years, both of
them devoted Christians long before their encounter with Frank Buchman,
but in his brilliant revolutionary way of acting out the real message I
think they found their long searched for expression and realization of
God's plan in an active community.

But things did not work out long term for us as a family. My father was
a writer and a journalist all his life and worked mainly in one of the
two dominant newspapers in Sweden. He had a deep spiritual experience in
his youth which remained his inspiration and the very core of his soul
and life. He has written hundreds of poems and he was studying Indian philosophy and religion long before people in general here were interested. I remember my father
telling me that real depth of thought was to be found in the Indian
traditions. Not that I understood at that time what it meant, but his
convictions impressed me. My father has written a very good,
concentrated book about Frank Buchman with the title "Experiment with
God" not translated into English. He lived to be
100 years and was a source of knowledge and philosophy for my children
too.

My parents left the community of MRA when the movement seem to have
taken a different direction in some ways from what it used to be
regarding "the way to go about world-revolution." Perhaps in some
European countries focus became more concentrated on changing the world
by convincing so called important people and less on the individual
introvert revolution and extrovert commitment to a new way of life. I do
not know what really happened, but I know that my father disliked the
loudness of the big drum in the advertising and in the anti-communism,
the American dominance and simplistic political influence - tendencies
which did not originate in Buchman's message in his understanding of it.

I do not know. But MRA disappeared from my life and my mother who had
found her life's meaning in Caux, became isolated and bitter about my
father's decision to leave. There was a heavy atmosphere in our home for
many years.

My late husband was a sikh from the Punjab. He left for eternity in
2009. We got three children. One of them, my second son Max Anup Singh,
now 57 years old, asked me to write this letter to you! Let me explain:

Max is a searcher after Truth and has been a full-timer in that calling
for the last 5-6 years. Earlier he was a successful entrepreneur and
built up a company working with children and young people who need more
than what the ordinary main-stream Swedish society has to offer, which
often is a narrow diagnosis beneficial only for Big Pharma and
neglecting basic human needs. Max started a new kind of school in
Stockholm and many other activities for young people and the need for a
new thinking about education became obvious as his organisation
grew. Still he was not fulfilled with his task and after twenty years of
hard work, he sold his company and set off on a different journey: the
search for the Meaning of Life, the Mystery of the Unknown, the
exploration of the human condition, the suppressed thirst for cosmic
connection. His journey has been adventurous and different from what
most people seem to even wish for in a life-time, the crowning
experiences of it happening in his own inner world. He travelled all
over and got in contact with indigenous peoples in several parts of the
world and changed his habits and life-style. The big money he got when
selling the company he spent on different attempts to get people
together for a common goal - to create a better world. No big results,
no real soul-mates, no giving up. He also looked deeper into his own
heritage on his father's side and studied Sikhism, visited the Golden
Temple in Amritsar, read Guru Nanak and contacted sikhs in Sweden. He
spent some time in the Amazonas and joined the Daimi Church, learned to
sing their songs in Portuguese and ended up leading ceremonies of this
kind in Sweden. One of his best friends, a Zen teacher invited him to
Mexico where he was introduced to the north american Plants of the Gods, and met many great and genuine seekers after Truth, but no guru to follow.

God has a plan! As a mother it has happened that I felt worry about the
wilderness and intensity in Max' yearning for the High and Unreachable, his boundless craving for new challenges and
expansions of the soul. But I have now left all anxiety behind and trust
fully in God's guidance in his life. Let me explain again:

About one and a half year ago I asked Max to visit an old childhood
friend of mine who lives in a town where he happened to be at the time.

This friend of mine and myself, both 16 years of age, spent one summer
in Caux 1958. She became an artist, a painter and still is. She has
remained committed to what she found in Caux and has genuinely praticed
and lived the message that Frank Buchman offered the World. I lost
contact with her many years ago but to my own surprise this thought
struck me that Max should meet her!

He did. And he was impressed. He had read my father's book about
Buchman, now he started studying it. I never talked to my children about
MRA when they grew up, or ever. I simply did not understand it myself
and had never experienced any joy in it during childhood. I always
believed in God and always knew we are eternal beings but it was only
when Max introduced me to Soma that I had a spiritual experience four
years ago, for which I am deeply grateful.

So Max looked up some more persons in Sweden who also had grown up with
parents in MRA-groups. Together we recently visited a couple Gunnar and
Sylvi Söderlind, whom I think you also know. They lived a long part of
their life in the MRA centerhouse in Stockholm as full-timers. To me it
was so great an event to meet this wonderful couple, talk heart-to-heart
about the essential things in life and listen to their experiences. For
me this meeting was like given back the lost joy of my childhood in a
heart-warming family way - and I am 84 years! The Söderlind family had
to start their life all over again from scratch when the MRA-house i
Sweden was sold because of stagnation and conflicts. They had no
money of course, no savings, but with continued Guidance they got a
place to live, a farm-house which through hard work they could use as a
bed-and-breakfast sort of enterprise. Now they also have cows, they grow
vegetables and their garden is beautiful! Apart from these daily duties,
they devote full-time energy and concentration and 
devotion to their documentation of all that happened in the MRA-movement
after 1945 - and that is not little! I love them both!

This summer I read the book "The Oxford Group, Group Revivalism" by
Anders Jarlert, which deals about the years 1930-1945 in Europe.

What all happened and happens in India?

Max also opened his eyes for art and for what art can be when inspired
by the Holy Spirit and in service of the Unified Divine. His awakened
ear for music, not least Indian music guides him to inner stillness and
connection in Cosmos (since there are no words for the Great Mystery I
have to use the torn and worn-out words until a new language for Tat
Twam Asi as real human experience will explode).

In spring this year Max started planning for a week-end gathering of
people interested in, seriously looking for, expressing need for, being on their way to, having the willingness for, an openess
of mind towards: Self-reflection in the light of honesty, purity,
unselfishness and love. The Human Being, God's own children meant to be
Co-Creators are far superior to any - any! - technical
invention, however advanced. Now at the last moment at the end of
Kaliyoga we have our last chance to re-connect with our Creator and the
Destiny of Humanity.

So on the 4th of July this summer we met in a wonderful place, an island
in the heart of Sweden where Max (- again a miracle! -) has been given
an old farm as a gift from heaven (no money involved!) where he can
invite people for great events, and eventually also for courses in
Yoga - he is advanced in Kaliyoga after many years of practise.

So, 4th of July: Max and friends had worked hard to clean and empty the
enormous barn for the art-exhibition, the paintings, the handicrafts,
the weaving-pieces and mats, the carvings - and decorated with
birchtrees and flowers the old wooden barn, spacious and shining as if
happy to be used again after more than hundred years - looked like the
right Place for the right People to meet! Max had invited
good friend from the Amazonas, who must be the best
guitarist in the world (next to my youngest son Lucas, of course!) and a great singer, former priest in the Daimi Church - to come
to Sweden for this occasion. And he came, thanks to God! Many wonderful
people turned up, a colourful mix of personalities, old and young,
families and also locals from the island where the farm is. We had
advertised the exhibition under the name of "Reshape the World"
Grandiosity in a simple barn from long ago! Around 35 people took their
seats on the ground floor, facing the enormous heavy doors on the stable
side with a cross painted in red from the floor up to the roof. Standing
in front Max asked for quiet time during 15 minutes of silence. It was
absolute. Swallows flew in and out over our heads, gracefully
contributing stillness to our soul with their wings. After the quiet
quarter of an hour the guitarist from the Amazonas started playing. I
never heard anything like it. When he finished Max opened the gigantic
doors - like portals - in front of us and we looked out onto the wide
open landscape of nordic summer, the blooming meadows, the blue
mountains far and the Viking Graves nearby, our
ancestors heritage from long ago.

Max made a short speech about the acute catastrophic situation in the
world and the need for change before it is too late for humanity to
survive. He said a few words about the crucial importance of
self-investigation purification, courage to kill the ego
and take responsibility for our life.

More music, magic singing followed. Art was presented. I had the task of
presenting the paintings which my childhood friend had given us! So full
of light and life that the barn was gleaming in their radiance. I told
about her lifelong warmhearted dedication to Frank Buchman's message,
also expressed in some poems of her, which I read aloud.

In another part of the barn, we exhibited the dark side of our human
history. Some simple symbols of the decay and deviation from Natural
Laws and separation from Higher Intelligence. We entered the room in
silence. After some time Max said a few words about the guilt of the
Western civilisation as Conquerors, Colonisers and missionary destroyers
of indigenous cultures, as greedy exploiters of Mother Earth - all in
the name of Christendom.

The holy Cow was exposed there in the dark, a sculpture made of paper,
bleeding and violated, the symbol of Mother Earth.

Our beloved guitarist and singer from the threatened rainforest in the
Amazonas sang his last song for the day, and there was light again! I
cried and I was not the only one.

The atmosphere opened for communication and people were intensively
sharing thoughts in groups, looking alive. I experienced a kind of being
socially spontaneous, on a serious ground, above the
usual egotistical intuitions, a level of togetherness where laughter
sounds genuine and conversation cease to be only a cover for real
contact.

Max two grown up children were taking part and told me afterwards that
this had been the best week-end in their life. The forth generation
receiving a glimps of Buchman's message with an open heart!

Max has some times felt divided in his ancestral heritage
Swedish-Christian and Indian. How to combine, be whole, come Home at
last? No more divided, is not either - or, it is all
together that becomes more! So not long ago we heard from our friends
from MRA about your center in India. We so much want to meet you and to
visit your center! It is our greatest wish - the wish of my eternal
parents, my wish, the wish of my son Max, the wish of his two children
27 and 20 years of age. And also the wish of my child-hood friend Gerd
Ekdahl, the artist and some other friends, Seekers After Truth.
